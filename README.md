<h1 align="center">OpenEPCIS Video Series: Learn GS1 EPCIS with Open-Source Tools</h1>

A weekly video series on GS1 EPCIS, the Core Business Vocabulary (CBV) and the Digital Product Passport (DPP). Each episode explains one topic in plain words and shows it live in the open-source OpenEPCIS tools.

This repository holds the slides, links and short notes for every episode.

## Who it is for

Developers, supply chain and traceability teams, and anyone who has to send or receive EPCIS data and wants a practical start. No prior EPCIS knowledge is needed for the first episodes.
